Shrink a SoundFont

Pick an SF2 file and get a bank that fits the PurrMIDI flash (8,384,512 bytes or less). Nothing is uploaded: everything runs in your browser.

Advanced

What happens to the bank

  1. Stereo becomes mono. The right channel is first aligned in time and polarity to the left, so notes do not cancel when summed, and loudness is normalised to keep the power of the pair.
  2. Resampling. Samples above the chosen rate are converted with a windowed-sinc filter. Samples already at a lower rate are left as they are.
  3. Loops stay seamless. The loop length at the new rate is a whole number of samples, and the tiny pitch shift (fractions of a cent) is compensated with the pitchCorrection field.
  4. Everything else is unchanged: presets, instruments, key and velocity ranges, root keys, envelopes. Output is 16-bit.

It is the same algorithm as the SoundFonts/sf2_shrink.py script, and automated tests compare their output. The script is better for very large banks and batch work.

Limits

  • Banks whose zones use sample address offsets (start/end/loop address offsets) are not supported: they lose their meaning after resampling. Some Florestan banks are like this. For those, use Polyphone and delete unneeded presets and instruments.
  • SF3 banks (Ogg Vorbis) and banks with ROM samples are not supported.
  • The bank becomes mono: the stereo image is lost and the instrument sounds from the centre. 24-bit extensions (sm24) are dropped, so the output is 16-bit.
  • The file is processed entirely in browser memory. Very large banks (hundreds of MB) are better shrunk with the script.
